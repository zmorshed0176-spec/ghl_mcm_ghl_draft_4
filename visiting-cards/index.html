Card details

Size & setup

85 × 55 mm, with 3 mm bleed on every side and text kept 4 mm inside the trim. Every option is laid out on that grid, and the smallest type on any card is about 6 pt.

Logo & orange for print

Foil and deboss need a vector logo file (SVG, AI or PDF), not a PNG. Send your printer the logo's Pantone code too, and ask to see an ink or foil sample before the full run.

Tactile finishes

Raised stripes, dots and deep pressing need thick cotton or duplexed board (600 gsm or more) so the impression doesn't show through on the other side.
