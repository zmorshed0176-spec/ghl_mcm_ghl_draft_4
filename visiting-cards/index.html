Card details

Size & setup

85 × 55 mm, 3 mm bleed on all sides, keep text 4 mm inside the trim. Artwork for every option is built on that grid.

Getting the orange right

Screens and paper show orange differently. Send your printer the Pantone code from your logo guide and ask for an ink or foil drawdown before the full run.

Tactile finishes

Deboss, emboss and letterpress need thick cotton or duplexed board (600 gsm and up) so the impression doesn't show through to the other side.
